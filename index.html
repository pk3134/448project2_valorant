HUD reaction test

Measures how fast players notice a HUD warning, comparing the current Valorant HUD with our redesign.

Version

Read this to the tester

  1. Keep your eyes on the crosshair in the middle of the screen.
  2. Small targets will pop up next to the crosshair. Click the mouse or press Enter to shoot them.
  3. Something on the HUD will change after a few seconds.
  4. Press Space as soon as you notice the change.
  5. Then pick what you noticed (keys 1–5 or click).

Pressing Space too early counts as a false start, and that trial starts again.
